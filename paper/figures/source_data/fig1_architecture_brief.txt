Figure 1 source brief

Claim: FocalSwap converts behavior-level regimes into a search-centered audit row,
five controlled views, gate tests, and separated evidence readouts.

Architecture elements:
- Regimes: KuaiSAR anonymous behavior S->R logs; Qilin real query text lexical scorer.
- Search-centered row: pre-search history, focal search, post-search outcome,
  leakage-safe candidate row, same-user ordered time window.
- Controlled views: Full-SAR, History-only, Search-only, Search-masked,
  Counterfactual-search.
- Evidence tests: G1 establishes outcome availability. G3 reports target-rank
  response, signed contribution, and focal specificity under fixed-row
  interventions. In parallel, G2 calibrates History-only/Search-only routing
  opportunity and G4 tests input-side recovery.
- Evidence readout: ranking utility, intervention response, signed
  contribution, focal specificity, routing opportunity, and recovery kept
  separate.

Scope: S->R FocalSwap architecture only. Feed2Search/R->S content is intentionally
not represented.
